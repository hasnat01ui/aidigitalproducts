## Rules

1. **Two genuinely good articles beat eight thin ones.** Thin content is penalised and this audience spots filler immediately.
2. Every article ships with metadata, canonical, one `h1`, internal links, and a CTA.
3. Never publish a claim that isn't true. No invented statistics, no fabricated case studies.
4. Once the audit has real data, revisit #1 and #3 and replace estimates with our own numbers — that is the version that earns links.
