## What the scores say

The top ideas cluster tightly around one thing: **non-billable work in the client lifecycle** — winning it (2, 8, 12), starting it (3, 6), running it (4, 10), and keeping it (13). That is not a coincidence; it is the market gap identified in `market-gaps.md` showing up independently in the product scoring.

Note idea **19 (Aurevia OS)** scores well on value and recurring revenue but bottoms out on ease and time-to-market. That is the correct signal: it is the right *destination* and the wrong *starting point*, exactly as the brief's product ladder anticipates.

Note idea **9 (free audit)** scores poorly on profitability by design — it is a lead magnet. Its 10/10 upsell score is the point.

## The recommended product ladder

Rather than picking one idea, the top-scoring ideas assemble into a coherent ladder. Each stage is a real product, and each is only built once the previous one is validated.

### Stage 0 — Free lead magnet
**Idea #9 — Agency Time-Recovery Audit**
A genuinely useful instrument that lets an owner measure how many hours their agency loses to non-billable work, and where. Delivers a real answer standalone.

*Strategic role:* it makes the buyer measure their own pain in their own numbers, which is far more persuasive than any claim AUREVIA could make — and it is the honest version of urgency the brand is allowed to use. It also produces the baseline against which the paid product's ROI is later measured, supporting Gap 6.

### Stage 1 — Starter product (~$79)
**Idea #2 — AI Proposal Engine**
One painful, high-frequency, immediately monetary task: turning a messy brief into a scoped, priced, on-brand proposal.

*Strategic role:* lowest-friction first purchase. Proves AUREVIA delivers before asking for a larger commitment. Highest demand score of any paid idea.

### Stage 2 — Core product (~$299)
**Idea #1 — Agency Non-Billable Recovery System**
The flagship, absorbing ideas #3, #4, #5, #6, #8, and #13 into one system covering the client lifecycle end to end: win → onboard → deliver → report → renew.

*Strategic role:* the product AUREVIA is actually about. Highest total score. This is where the method, the team-adoption design, and the quality gates live.

### Stage 3 — Premium bundle (~$799)
Core product plus team training (#14), automation blueprints (#17), and implementation support.

*Strategic role:* captures the buyers who want help, without AUREVIA becoming a consultancy.

### Stage 4 — Aurevia Pro (~$49/month)
**Idea #7**
Ongoing updates as models change, new workflows monthly, member library, member-only resources.

*Strategic role:* converts a one-time sale into recurring revenue, and turns the model-churn problem (Gap 4) from a liability into the subscription's core justification.

### Stage 5 — Aurevia OS
**Idea #19**
Software that encodes the validated method. Built only after the method has been proven by paying customers — never before.

## The immediate build decision

**Build Stage 0 and Stage 1 first, in that order, and validate before touching Stage 2.**

Rationale: the free audit (#9) is fast to produce, tests the core assumption that agency owners believe non-billable work is their bottleneck, and builds the email list. The Proposal Engine (#2) is a small paid product that tests willingness to pay at low risk. Only if both signals are positive does the flagship system (#1) justify weeks of build effort.

This sequencing directly de-risks validation assumptions #1, #2, and #5 from `research/customer-avatar.md` before the expensive work begins.

See `validation/validation-plan.md` for the pass/fail thresholds.
